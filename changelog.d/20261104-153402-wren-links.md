**Links that open in Wren**: a web page can link to `wren-https://...`, `wren-http://...` or `wren-about:wren`, and the link opens in Wren (a new tab of the Wren that runs, else a new Wren) with its `wren-` prefix taken away. Setup and Repair register the three schemes (Windows: `HKCU\Software\Classes`; Linux: the desktop entry's MIME types), the uninstall removes them. Only http and https addresses and about:wren come out of a link: any other scheme, a control character or a link over 8 KiB opens nothing. `wren.exe --open-url <link>` is what the system runs (`docs/desktop-integration.md`).
