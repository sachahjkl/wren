**Engine BigInt in Odin** (`src/engine/bigint`): arbitrary-precision integers for the JavaScript engine, written natively — sign-magnitude u32 limbs with the ECMAScript two's-complement bit semantics layered on top. Add, subtract, Karatsuba multiply, Knuth Algorithm D division and modulo (truncating toward zero), power, left/right shifts, `& | ^ ~`, signed comparison, `asIntN` / `asUintN`, and conversion to and from f64 and from/to strings in every base 2..36. The allocator is injected through `context.allocator`. Verified over 12M+ random operations against native i128/u128 for small operands and against algebraic identities (round trips, divmod reconstruction, Karatsuba vs schoolbook) for operands up to several thousand bits. Builds for `windows_amd64`, `linux_amd64` and `freestanding_wasm32`.
