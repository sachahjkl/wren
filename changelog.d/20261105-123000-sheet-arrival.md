**A stylesheet that arrives restyles what it matches, not the document** (`style.incremental.sheets`): its rules join those in force, numbered in its place among the sheets, the invalidation index grows by them, and only the elements its selectors' keys find are restyled; the same for a rule a script inserts at the end of a sheet (CSS-in-JS). A sheet that came on cnn.com costs about 14 ms instead of about 250. `@layer`, `@property`, `@import`, `::part()` and the like, and sheets that match most of the document, still make a full pass. A `<link>` or `<style>` element that comes back where it was when the parser reveals it no longer makes one.
