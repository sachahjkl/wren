**A mouse at 1 kHz no longer makes a thousand frames a second on a variable refresh screen** (`pacing.odin`, toolkit 0.36.2): on such a screen the swap chain's latency object is released at once, so nothing paced the browser, and a frame woken by input skipped the timer: 270 to 915 frames presented per second, 1 ms apart, measured while dragging a tab on a 180 Hz G-Sync monitor. Every frame now waits for its slot (`platform.Frame_Slots`), only the one that starts an answer to input (after a rest) and a resize step are made at once: at most one frame per period, 5.6 ms at 180 Hz. Where the screen's refresh does pace the frames the grid is a loose safety net that never delays one (and tightens by itself after 8 delayed frames in a row). `wren ctl stats` says `hzReported`, `hzMax`, `hzNow`, `hzSwapChain`, `variableRefresh`, `framePeriodMs`, `frameCapStrict`; `tools\display_probe.ps1` reads the screen three ways.
