**The accessibility tree of a page follows its changes node by node** (`a11y.live_update`, `platform.a11y_diff_patch`): after the first whole tree, only the nodes a change reaches are written, found from the document's mutation journal, the style journal, the forms, the listeners and the geometry; nothing is built or sent while nothing changes. New preferences `accessibility.page.enabled`, `accessibility.page.incremental`, `accessibility.page.verify` (every update compared with a whole tree), `accessibility.page.max_nodes`, `accessibility.page.frames`; `wren ctl stats` tells builds, patches, frames and differences (`a11yPage*`).
