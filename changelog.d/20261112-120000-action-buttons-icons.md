**Buttons with an icon and a colour, as the Vault's**: the buttons of the browser's pages, dialogs, panels and banners (page information, QR code, downloads, bookmarks, history, search engines, preferences, desktop integration, the crash banner, the notification, storage access and file system access prompts, the blocker's panel, element picker and dashboard) show an icon. Their colour says what they do: one colour to confirm, one to remove or block, one to cancel or close, one to show more.
