**CSS Text Decoration 3 and 4, whole**: `text-decoration-skip-ink` (underlines leave gaps around the ink of glyphs, not of CJK text unless `all`, from their outlines), `text-underline-position`, `text-decoration-thickness` and `text-underline-offset` from the font and in percentages, `text-decoration-skip-spaces`, `text-decoration-inset`, `text-emphasis` and its longhands, `-webkit-text-stroke` and the `spelling-error` and `grammar-error` lines. Each box that decorates text keeps its own line, style, colour and thickness at its own baseline; underlines and overlines are under the text, line-throughs over it, and `text-shadow` shadows them all, in rows, columns and right to left.
