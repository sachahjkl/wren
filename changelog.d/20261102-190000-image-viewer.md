**An image opened by itself is a viewer**: Ctrl+wheel (and a touchpad's pinch) zooms the image around the pointer, + and - zoom around the center and 0 fits it again; a click toggles fit and 100 %; the image is dragged with the pointer (grab, grabbing), moved with the wheel and the arrows when it is larger than the window; R and Shift+R (and the context menu) turn it a quarter; a double click resets the view. Ctrl+Plus / Minus / 0 still zoom the page.
