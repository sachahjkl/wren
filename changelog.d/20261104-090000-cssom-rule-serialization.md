**Stylesheet rules read back as other browsers write them**: a rule's `cssText`, `selectorText`, `conditionText` and `media.mediaText` are CSSOM's serialization of the rule as parsed, not its source text (`div>p{margin:0}` reads `div > p { margin: 0px; }`, `@media ALL and (MIN-WIDTH:10px)` reads `@media (min-width: 10px)`), which CSS-in-JS libraries, web component frameworks and devtools-like tools copy and compare. `CSSMediaRule`, `CSSSupportsRule`, `CSSImportRule`, `CSSPageRule`, `CSSKeyframeRule`, `CSSNamespaceRule`, `CSSPropertyRule`, `CSSMarginRule`, `CSSLayerStatementRule` and `MediaList` exist for `instanceof` and give their members; a `@keyframes` rule lists its keyframes (`length`, `findRule`, `keyText`); setting a top-level style rule's `selectorText` changes what it selects.
