**A sibling that comes or goes restyles what depends on it, not what stands beside it**: what reads an element's place among its siblings (`:last-child`, `:nth-child()`, `+`, `~`) is looked at by the class, id or tag its compound requires, from where the change was, and what a rule can restyle under an element is found by its subject whether it is a class, an attribute or the alternatives of an `:is()`. A banner added to or removed from `<body>` on cnn.com marked a third of the document and made a full pass: its 18 such passes are gone.
