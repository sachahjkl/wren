**Fonts on Linux**: a page's `"Courier New"`, `monospace`, `serif` or `sans-serif` gets the font the system configures for it (fontconfig's aliases and generic families, gui_toolkit), and the defaults of `font.family.*` are fontconfig's generic names there. Such text came out in the interface's Roboto: the ASCII art of a page in `"Fira Mono", "Courier New", monospace` had spaces a third as wide as its blocks.
