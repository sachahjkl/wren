**The licence card's lettering on Linux** (about:wren): the name, the e-mail, the date and the order number, the seal's word and the line on the back are raised from the glyphs' outlines (`font.glyph_outline`, filled by `font.colr_cover`) instead of the glyph atlas's bitmaps, which only Windows has (off Windows Slug draws text from the outlines, and the atlas held nothing for the card: it showed its art, its seal and its logo but no text).
