**The event loop and the network take no lock**: a post to the loop goes through a lock-free queue (`src/host/mpsc.odin`: blocks of slots claimed with one compare-and-swap, each producer's order kept, a slot still being written ends the turn and its producer wakes the next), the loop sleeps on its wake flag itself (one wake is one wake: a wait no longer returns for the posts of a turn that already ran them), the network's completions reach the loop through the same queue (`host.Inbox`: one drain posted per burst, refused once the client is closing), and `parallel_for` hands a round to its threads with one atomic word instead of a mutex and a condition. A thread that posts can no longer hold up the window's thread by being descheduled in the middle of a post. The inventory of the locks and the numbers: `docs/performance.md`.
