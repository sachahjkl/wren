**A window that moves to a monitor of another scale changes scale at once and alone** (`tab_scale_follow`, `page_scale`, `favicon_decode_px`) : its page keeps its place in the document (the scroll, in device pixels, jumped by the ratio of the scales; a tab moved to a window of another scale did the same), its page is laid out at the new scale from the first frame (not the last build's), the other windows keep theirs, the developer tools' window draws at its own monitor's scale, tab icons are decoded for the largest scale of the windows (and again when it grows). `wren ctl window --dpi n` simulates the move (the system's `WM_DPICHANGED`).
