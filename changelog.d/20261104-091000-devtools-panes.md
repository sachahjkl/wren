**Developer tools: panes you size, fold and place.** The pane at the side of the Elements tree (Styles, Computed, Layout, Event Listeners, Accessibility) can be dragged from a few characters wide to nearly the whole panel (it was held between 20 % and 80 %), folded by a double click on its bar, by Enter on it, by dragging it shut or by the chevron at the start of its tabs, and brought back by a click on the thin strip it leaves; its place is at the side, below, or chosen by the room (the button next to the chevron). A right click on a tab moves it to a drawer under the others (or back), as Chrome's drawer; the settings menu shows the Console there in one click; the drawer is resized, folded and closed the same way. The panel docked under or beside the page goes from a header and a row to all the window but a strip of the page (it was held to 70 %); a floating one shrinks further. The Network tab's request details and the Application tab's value pane are resized by their bars (the network bar's drag ran away from the pointer; the value pane's bar did nothing). The sizes and folds are kept in the profile (`browser.devtools.elements.sidebar`, `browser.devtools.drawer`, `browser.devtools.drawer_tabs`, `browser.devtools.drawer_tab`).
