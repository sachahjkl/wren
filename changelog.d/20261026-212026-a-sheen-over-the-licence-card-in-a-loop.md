**A sheen over the licence card, in a loop**: a band of light crosses the card every 7 seconds, and what it lights is the relief of the card, not a flat surface. The card has a height field (a guilloche of two rosettes of wavy rings and a bevelled frame, `about_card_relief.odin`) from which three pictures are made when the page is built, as a normal map is used: the surface lit from the top left, and the slopes that face the sheen and those that turn their back to it, which are the masks (`mask-image`) of two bands of light, a long cool tail behind it and a short warm glow ahead of it, plus a prismatic line at its centre (`color-dodge`). The ridges of the guilloche glint as it passes. It rests under the pointer (the tilt takes over) and is left out with `prefers-reduced-motion`. Native: CSS animation and masks, no script.
