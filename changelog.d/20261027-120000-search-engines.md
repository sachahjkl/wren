**A list of search engines, and the default one chosen in it** (`browser.search.engine`, about:config category Search, `search_engines.txt` in the profile): built-in engines (DuckDuckGo, Bing, Google, Qwant, Ecosia, Startpage, Wikipedia) and engines added, edited, reordered or removed by the user, each with a name, an address with `%s` and an optional keyword; the default is a choice of their names. A profile of before keeps its choice (`custom` and `browser.search.custom_url` become an engine "Custom"). See docs/search-engines.md.
  - Address bar: a keyword first (`@g cats`, `k rust`) searches with that engine; the search suggestion reads "Search with <engine>", Ctrl+Down / Ctrl+Up picks another engine for this time, and a row of engine badges under the suggestions searches with the one clicked (`browser.urlbar.suggest.engines`).
  - Context menu: "Search <default> for …" follows the default, and a "Search with" submenu offers the others.
