**`wrentool replay`**: a bench that can prove a change of a few percent. `record` keeps what a site served for a load, `run` serves those bytes from a local server to a page with a seeded random and a fixed clock (test-only: `WREN_TEST_WORLD`), and says per load when the first viewport was ready, when the window thread settled, what it spent until then, and the load events beside them. Two more ways in for it: `*=address:port` in `network.hosts`, and `action: frames` in `Wren.timeline`.
