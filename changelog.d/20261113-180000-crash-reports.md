**Crash reports in the toolkit's format**: a crash leaves `crash-<date>-<time>-<pid>.txt` in the profile's `crashes` folder with every thread's stack, the modules and the preferences you changed (a minidump beside it on Windows), and a session that ended without one is reported at the next start as `unclean-...txt`. The crash recovery bar's Details button opens the newest. Nothing is sent anywhere.
