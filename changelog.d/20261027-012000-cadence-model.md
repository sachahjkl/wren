type: Removed

**Pacing switches replaced by the cadence model** (`src/browser/pacing.odin`, toolkit `platform.frame_decision`, its `docs/pacing.md`): `browser.frame.vblank_clock`, `input_immediate`, `input_idle_periods`, `video_at_vblank`, `resize_immediate`, `resize_hold_ms` and `resize_skip_echoes` are gone. What they chose at their defaults is now the model's own behaviour: each frame is in one state (`Rest`, `Continuous`, `Drag`, `Input_Answer`, `Resize_Step`, `Resize_Burst`) and one pure decision says when it starts, whether it waits for the screen and whether it is shown at once. A profile that still sets them is read without error and they change nothing. The real choices stay: `browser.frame.target_hz`, `max_hz`, `present_mode`, `variable_refresh`. `wren ctl stats` adds `frameStates` (frames per state) and `frameState`.
