**Intl in Odin** (`native/js/odin/intl.odin`, `api_decl/intl.odin`): the engine's minimal ECMA-402 (NumberFormat, DateTimeFormat, PluralRules, RelativeTimeFormat, ListFormat, Collator, Locale, Segmenter, DisplayNames, DurationFormat, and the `toLocale*` / `localeCompare` methods) leaves `runtime/intl.c` with the same output; the browser's time zone is the one declared host call, bound through `jsb`, and integers are written through `numconv`.
