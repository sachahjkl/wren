Storage partitioning (`storage.partitioned`, on by default; replaces `frames.storage_partitioning`): what a site stores while embedded in another site is kept per top-level site: cookies (a partition key per cookie, kept on disk), localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, the Cache API, OPFS and buckets, service worker registrations, BroadcastChannel, shared workers. `document.hasStorageAccess()` / `requestStorageAccess()` (after a click in the frame; `storage.access_requests` ask / allow / deny, a prompt over the page), `Sec-Fetch-Storage-Access` on cross-site credentialed requests; a cross-site frame's `document.cookie` is a cross-site context. "Clear site data" and about:sites cover the Cache API, service workers, cookie partitions and the partitions a site held as the top level.
