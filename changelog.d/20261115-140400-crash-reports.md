**New crash report files**: a crash leaves `crash-<date>-<time>-<pid>.txt` in the profile's `crashes` folder, with the stack of every thread, the loaded modules and the settings you changed. On Windows a minidump is written beside it. A session that ended without a report is reported at the next start as `unclean-<date>-<time>-<pid>.txt`. The Details button of the crash recovery bar opens the newest report. Nothing is sent anywhere.
