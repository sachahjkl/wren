**Freed memory made loud** (`-define:WREN_POISON_MEMORY=true`, `src/base/poison.odin`: `build.bat check`, `./build.sh wren-check`, the end-to-end tests): what is freed is filled with a pattern and kept out of the heap for a while, for the Odin heap, arenas, scopes, temporary allocators, pool slots and the C heap of the native libraries (the executable's `HeapFree` and `HeapReAlloc` on Windows, `free` and `realloc` on Linux); a block that grows always moves. A pointer read from freed memory faults at once, a double free or a write to a freed block ends the process with a crash report. Normal builds have nothing of it. `test_all` and the Linux job of the CI visit the pages of `tools/sweep_local.txt` with it (`wrentool sweep`, no network); a crash report says when the address 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF is not canonical.
