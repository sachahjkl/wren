**Alike elements share their selector matching**: siblings with the same tag and attributes, and their cousins, take what the first of them matched and match again only the selectors that tell them apart (their position, their state, their content, an ancestor's), so a full style pass of a page made of repeated items costs a quarter to nearly two fifths less (a page that rebuilds a list of 2 500 items every frame: 1.8 ms a pass instead of 3.0, and 92 frames/s instead of 86). A partial layout that would end up rebuilding too much finds it out before looking for what to rebuild (that page's layout wait: 3.3 ms instead of 4.7). The numbers: `docs/performance.md`.
