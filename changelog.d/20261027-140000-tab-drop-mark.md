**The mark of a tab carried over another window's tabs is where the release puts it** (`drop_slot_row`, `drop_slot_list`, `window_drop_slot`) : among pinned tabs (a pinned tab goes among them, another after them), between two tabs of a group (it joins the group: the mark takes its colour), in the sidebar's list with its group headers and collapsed groups (the rows were taken as all of one height and the pinned tabs as rows of the list); the window that gets the drop is the topmost one under the pointer (a window covering another's tabs there hides them). `wren ctl drag --hold` / `--resume` hold a drag on its way (to capture the mark), `wren ctl windows --json` gives a window's `drop` (index, group, mark) and its client area's place; a drag of injected input follows its own positions, not the system's cursor (`platform.Event.injected`).
