**`AudioWorklet`**: `audioContext.audioWorklet.addModule()`, `AudioWorkletGlobalScope` with `registerProcessor`, `AudioWorkletProcessor` and `AudioWorkletNode` with `parameters`, `port` and `processorerror`, in realtime and offline contexts. The processors run in a realm on a thread of their own; a quantum waits for it with a deadline and is silence on a miss, and a realm that throws, stops or hangs (`webaudio.worklet_stall_ms`) is `processorerror`, never a hung render thread.
