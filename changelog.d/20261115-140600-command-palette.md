**The command palette ranks its results differently and starts a new memory of your choices**:
  - A title that holds the typed words whole, or the start of its words, comes first. Then come the other words of a command and its group, then the same matches with one or two typing mistakes. Letters scattered inside a word no longer match.
  - The commands and settings you choose are now kept in `palette_choices.txt` of the profile. The old `palette_usage.txt` is not read: your most used commands come first again only after you use them again.
  - The keys in the menus and in the palette are written in a new form (`Ctrl+=`, `Escape`).
