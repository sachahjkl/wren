**The tabs come back after a crash, a kill or a power cut, and Wren never starts in a loop** (`browser.session.crash_recovery`, on by default, with `crash_snapshot_ms`, `crash_stable_ms` and `crash_backups`; `src/browser/crash_recovery.odin`, `crash_banner.odin`, [docs/crash-recovery.md](docs/crash-recovery.md)): a `running.txt` marker in the profile (process id and creation time) is written at the start and removed at a normal end; one found at the next start means the end was abnormal, unless the process it names still runs (the single-instance mutex or the process check says so: a Wren that is in use is never taken for a crash). All the tabs, with their histories, scroll positions and zoom, go to `recovery.txt` (the session file format, written atomically by a worker of the blocking pool a few seconds after a change, never on a throwaway profile). The next start opens them again under a bar (`Wren closed unexpectedly. Restored 3 tabs.` with `Details`, which opens the crash report, and `Start fresh`). A start that restores and ends again before 30 s does not: the following one opens a blank page, keeps the tabs in `crashed-session <date>.txt` and offers `Restore tabs` (or the command `Restore the tabs of the session that crashed`). `tools\crash_recovery_e2e.ps1` kills real Wren processes and checks all of it.
