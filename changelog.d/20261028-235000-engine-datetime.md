**Engine Date in Odin** (`src/engine/datetime`, package `jsdate`): the ECMAScript Date calendar and string handling, ported from QuickJS-ng to native Odin and verified bit-for-bit against QuickJS over 1.5M+ cases. Proleptic Gregorian `MakeDay`/`MakeTime`/`MakeDate`, `TimeClip`, time-value to and from broken-down fields, `toISOString` / `toString` / `toUTCString` / `toLocale*` formatting, and `Date.parse` (strict ISO 8601 plus the legacy browser format, timezone abbreviations and offsets). The local-time offset is a host callback (`timezone_offset_proc`, wired to `js.timezone_offset`); UTC math needs no host. Allocation-free; builds for `windows_amd64`, `linux_amd64` and `freestanding_wasm32`.
