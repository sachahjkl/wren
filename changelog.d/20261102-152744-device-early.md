**The graphics device is made on a thread of its own from the start** (`render.preload_device`, before the window, the fonts, the network and the first tab): the renderer takes it, then makes the swap chain (or the headless window's offscreen target, WARP when the test asks for software). The driver's first load in the process now runs beside the rest of the start instead of after it. Measured on the dev build (median of 5): first present (`wrentool bench startup`, two sets) 306 and 308 ms to 284 and 277 ms, the renderer's phase (`WREN_PHASES`) 192 ms to 148 ms; the headless start of the end-to-end tests to its first frame 235 ms to 222 ms. The WebGPU build makes its adapter and device early the same way.
