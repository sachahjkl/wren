**Legacy encodings, the rest of the way**: in a Shift_JIS page the IBM extensions (Roman numerals, `ⅰ`, `纊`...) go into links and forms as the IBM bytes (0xFA..0xFC) the standard and every server expect, not as NEC's duplicates; in GBK and gb18030 the private use code points GB18030-2022 gave to real characters encode as their bytes; `TextDecoder` and `charset` know the labels the standard added (`unicode`, `ucs-2`, `unicodefffe`, `unicode20utf8`...) and refuse a label padded with a vertical tab or a no-break space. A frame's document that declares no encoding takes its same-origin parent's (it was windows-1252), and a text or JSON document in a frame is decoded in its response's charset.
