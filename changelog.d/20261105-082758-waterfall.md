**`wrentool waterfall`**: where a first load's time goes, in a fresh Wren per page: the wait for a slot, name, connection, handshake, server and body of each request, the time before the load event with nothing in flight, first paint, DOMContentLoaded and load (`Wren.network` now says the protocol, the phases of each request and the page's marks). `wrentool sweep`: `load_ms` is the load; the wait for the page to calm down, up to 4 s after it and 400 ms at the least, is `settle_ms`.
