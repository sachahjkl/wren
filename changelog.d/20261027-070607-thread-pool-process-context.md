**The threads of a thread pool no longer allocate through the allocator of whoever made the pool** (`host.thread_pool_make`, `style.thread_arena_make`, `src/style/cascade.odin` `Pass.made_with`): they start from the process's context, since they outlive their creator and serve every caller of `parallel_for`. A pool made by a test shared the test's allocator (not thread-safe) between its threads: the style tests that run passes on several threads crashed one run in two (`parallel_pass_gives_the_sequential_result`, `restyle_dom_parallel`, `media_flip_on_several_threads_gives_the_full_result`: a rule pointer holding a node handle, a bounds trap on a pool thread); 13 runs out of 13 pass now, and under `-sanitize:address`. A style thread's pass and arena are freed with the allocator that made them.
