**`innerText` as other browsers give it**: images, canvases, videos, frames, text fields and other replaced elements give no text of their own (a canvas's fallback, a textarea's value) but keep the spaces around them, as inline-blocks do; flex and grid items and the options of a `<select>` are a line each; a closed `<details>` gives its summary only; text in SVG resources, a shadow tree's contents and white space between table parts are left out; the space before a `<br>` goes, and `ß` upper-cased by `text-transform` is `SS`. Selections copy the same way.
