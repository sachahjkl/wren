**Documents of their own keep their nodes**: an element made by a document of its own (`createHTMLDocument`'s, `new Document()`, `createDocument`'s, `DOMParser`'s), adopted (`adoptNode`) or imported (`importNode`) into one, or removed from one, has that document as `ownerDocument` (it was the page's until inserted), and its links encode their query in UTF-8 whatever the page's encoding; `createDocumentType` of such a document's `implementation` makes a doctype of it. `document.URL` and `documentURI` of `createHTMLDocument`'s, `createDocument`'s, `new Document()` and `parseHTMLUnsafe`'s documents are `about:blank`, not the page's.
