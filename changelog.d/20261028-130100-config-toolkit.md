**Preference registry moved to the toolkit** (`toolkit:config`): the typed registry behind about:config (the `Prefs`, the four layers, validation, prefix observers, atomic save, snapshots, the titles and search for the settings UI) now lives in the toolkit, a cross-application concern, so another application reuses it. Wren's `wb:config` stays as a thin alias that re-exports the whole surface, so all 129 callers write `config.Prefs`, `config.register`, `config.get_bool`, etc. unchanged; the `config.Def` tables each subsystem declares in its `settings.odin` stay in Wren. No behaviour or file-format change.
