**Fill from the keyboard**: with the Vault unlocked, Ctrl+Shift+L fills the page's only login, or lists its logins (`vault.fill.shortcut` changes the key). A focused user name, password or one-time-code field shows the list of the page's logins under it: Down moves into it, Enter fills, Escape closes it (`vault.fill.dropdown`). Locked, the list offers to unlock.
