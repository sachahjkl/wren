**The frame rate is the standard setting**: Settings › Rendering › Frame rate (`rendering.frame_rate`) and View › Frame rate choose the screen's rate, 30, 60, 120 or 240 a second, or no limit, with the same words as the other programs. `browser.frame.target_hz`, `max_hz`, `cap` and `cap_custom_hz` are gone (a custom rate with them).
