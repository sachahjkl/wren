type: Changed

**The command palette is the toolkit's, the same as in the other programs of the family**: Ctrl+K or Ctrl+Shift+P opens it in each window, and the first character chooses what it searches:
  - `>` the commands, `@` the window's tabs and the recently closed tabs, `/` the bookmarks and the visited pages of the history (Ctrl+Enter opens one in a new tab), `:` the settings of about:config (a switch flips and the palette stays open), `$` the Vault's items while it is unlocked.
  - Without a character it searches the commands, the tabs, the pages and the settings at once. With nothing typed it lists the commands you chose most, then all the commands under their groups. A command that cannot run now is greyed and comes last.
  - The bookmarks moved from `*` to `/`, and `/` now searches the whole history, not only the pages of the open tabs.
  - The palette is a dialog of the toolkit: it remembers its size.
  - The settings `browser.palette.ctrl_k`, `browser.palette.width_em`, `browser.palette.rows`, `browser.palette.max_results`, `browser.palette.show_disabled`, `browser.palette.remember`, `browser.palette.usage_max_entries`, `browser.palette.usage_half_life_days`, `browser.palette.open_ms` and `browser.palette.source_max_items` are gone. Ctrl+K always opens the palette, and the palette always remembers your choices (a private window remembers none).
