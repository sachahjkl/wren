The update channel is configurable: `browser.update.require_signature` (on by default) can be turned off for a channel of your own that does not sign (About Wren then says the update is UNSIGNED and only the length and SHA-256 are checked), next to `browser.update.url`, `browser.update.base_url` and `browser.update.public_key`. docs/updates.md describes how to run a channel.
