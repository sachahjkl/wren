**Popover API** (`popover`, `showPopover()`, `hidePopover()`, `togglePopover()`, `:popover-open`, `popovertarget` / `popovertargetaction`): auto, manual and hint popovers, the popover stack (an auto popover closes the others except its ancestors), light dismiss on a click outside, Escape, `beforetoggle` (cancelable when opening) and `toggle` events (`ToggleEvent` with `oldState`, `newState`, `source`; `<details>`'s `toggle` now has them too), focus given to an `autofocus` element and given back. The UA style shows a popover fixed and centred above the page (no top layer: a z-index above the page's); absolutely positioned boxes now size `width` / `height: fit-content` to their content.
