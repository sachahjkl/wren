**Navigation API** (`window.navigation`, `api/navigation.widl`, `native/js/runtime/web_navigation.c`): `entries()`, `currentEntry` (`NavigationHistoryEntry`: `url`, `key`, `id`, `index`, `sameDocument`, `getState()`, `dispose`), `updateCurrentEntry()`, `canGoBack` / `canGoForward`, `navigate()`, `reload()`, `traverseTo()`, `back()`, `forward()` with their `committed` / `finished` promises, and `transition`. Every navigation of the document fires `navigate` first (`NavigateEvent`: `navigationType`, `destination`, `canIntercept`, `userInitiated`, `hashChange`, `signal`, `info`): location changes, `history.pushState` / `replaceState`, `history.go`, links the user follows, the browser's Back and Forward on an entry of the document; a listener may cancel it or `intercept()` it (the document stays, its handlers run, then `navigatesuccess` or `navigateerror`; scroll and focus reset after the transition, `scroll()`), and a new navigation aborts the one in progress. `currententrychange` after each same-document commit. The entries and their states are the tab's session history: a document shown again from the back/forward cache sees the right current entry, and a state given to a navigation to another document is that document's entry state.
