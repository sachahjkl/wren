**Video keeps playing while a page is busy** (`browser.video.fast_path`): when a long script holds the interface thread, a video that fills its box opaquely keeps playing — its newest picture is drawn over the page's last frame at its box during the browser's chrome frames, recomposing only the video, with no script run and no re-layout (YouTube's periodic long task no longer freezes the picture). `auto` (on), `off`, or `verify` (a diagnostic that reports in the console when the fast path recomposes, to compare against `off`).
