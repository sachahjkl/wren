**Audio, video and text tracks** (`media.tracks.enabled`): `video.audioTracks`, `videoTracks` and `textTracks` with `AudioTrackList` / `VideoTrackList` / `TextTrackList` (EventTargets: `addtrack`, `removetrack`, `change`), `AudioTrack` / `VideoTrack` (id, kind, label, language, `enabled` / `selected`) built from the container's tracks, and text tracks from `<track>` or `addTextTrack`.
  - **Native WebVTT** (`src/media/webvtt.odin`): the whole file — signature, NOTE / REGION / STYLE blocks, cues with identifiers, timing and settings (`align`, `line`, `position`, `size`, `vertical`) and inline markup (`<b><i><u><c.class><v Name><lang><ruby><rt>`, internal timestamps). `TextTrack.mode`, `addCue` / `removeCue`, `VTTCue`, `TextTrackCueList`; active cues tracked by the player's clock (`cuechange`, cue `enter` / `exit`), with no script polling.
  - **Subtitles drawn natively** (`media.subtitles.enabled`): the cues of showing tracks are painted over the video (the `::cue` defaults — white on a translucent box — honouring the cue alignment).
