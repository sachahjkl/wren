**requestVideoFrameCallback** (`video.requestVideoFrameCallback` / `cancelVideoFrameCallback`): a callback runs once when a new video picture is actually presented on screen (the real presentation path, in step with `getVideoPlaybackQuality`), with the `VideoFrameCallbackMetadata` (`presentationTime`, `expectedDisplayTime`, `width`, `height`, `mediaTime`, `presentedFrames`, `processingDuration`). Native, no timer polling. `media.video_frame_callbacks.max` caps how many may wait on one video.
