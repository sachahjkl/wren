type: Fixed

**A program that resizes the window from outside (AltSnap) no longer waits a screen refresh at every step** (`browser.frame.resize_hold_ms`, 120 ms, `src/browser/pacing.odin`: `resize_burst`, `pacer_at_once`): after the frame that sees a new size, the frames that follow (the one that settles the step, a wake between two steps) were presented with a wait for the refresh, which the graphics driver spends asleep inside `Present` (50 % of the samples of a drag); the program's `SetWindowPos` is synchronous and waited for the thread all that time (16 ms per call, a third of the steps). Those frames are now shown at once like the resize step (and CPU time over 3 s of drag drops from about 3.0 s to 1.7 s). With `tools\altdrag_sim.ps1`, with the posted messages and the `WM_SIZING` of AltSnap: 500 of 500 steps in 3 s (186 to 220 before), `SetWindowPos` 0.5 ms median and 1 ms at p95 (16.5 and 17 before), p95 of the gap between two frames 5 ms (16 to 21).
