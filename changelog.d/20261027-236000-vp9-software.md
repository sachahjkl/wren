**Software VP9 decoder** (`media.software.vp9_fallback`): a built-in pure-Rust VP9 decoder (vp9dec, in `native/media`) stands in when the system has no VP9 decoder of its own, so YouTube's high resolutions play on a machine without the VP9 Store extension. The AV1 software decoder now also stands in on Windows the same way (`media.software.av1_fallback`). `canPlayType` / `MediaSource.isTypeSupported` report VP9 and AV1 as supported whenever either the system or the built-in decoder can decode them.
