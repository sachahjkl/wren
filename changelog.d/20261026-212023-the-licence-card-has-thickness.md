**The licence card has thickness**: a rim of flat faces (the four sides, the rounded corners in facets) shaded by orientation, so that the card looks like a bank card when it tilts; the CERTIFIED seal is a coin, a stack of relief layers (cast shadow, ridged gold edge, domed face, highlight that follows the pointer, embossed word and tick) with a milled rim of 32 facets.
