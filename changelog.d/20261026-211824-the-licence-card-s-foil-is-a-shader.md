**The licence card's foil is a shader** (`card-foil`, `src/browser/shaders/card_foil.wgsl`, `about_card_relief.odin`): the CSS glow of the sheen (eight layers of gradients, masks and blend modes, and 190 KB of PNG data: URLs in the page) is replaced by a shader that lights a normal map made natively when the card is first painted (a guilloche of two rosettes, a lipped frame with a groove, micro-lettering `WREN` in the lip, a grain; 880 x 556 RGBA8: normal, height, cavity): brushed-metal highlights (anisotropic), thin-film iridescence that follows the tilt, two lights (one sweeps across the card every 7 seconds and rests, one follows the pointer), glitter and a fresnel rim. The tilt script is shorter (it no longer sets `--ang`, `--gx`, `--gy`) and resets the tilt when the pointer leaves; `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps the card still with the light at rest.
