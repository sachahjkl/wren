**Screen readers read web pages**: the page is a tree of its own under the chrome's (`src/a11y`): roles of HTML and ARIA, accessible names and descriptions as the Accessible Name Computation says (labels, `alt`, captions, legends, `aria-labelledby`, content, `title`, placeholder), states (checked, pressed, selected, expanded, disabled, required, read only, level, "3 of 7", values of ranges, progress and meters), live regions (`aria-live`, `alert`, `status`) announced when they change, the focused element, tables with their headers and indexes, shadow trees; `display: none`, `aria-hidden`, `hidden` and `inert` left out. NVDA and Narrator can click, focus, type into fields, step ranges and scroll. Made again only when the page changed, sent incrementally. Test `examples/a11y/page.html` (unit tests of `src/a11y`, and in `tools\a11y_e2e.ps1`).
