**Sizes and times in the settings are typed and shown with their unit.** In about:config and on the settings pages, a size takes "4 MiB", "512 KiB" or "2 GB" and reads "64 MiB" instead of 67108864 bytes; a time takes "300 ms", "1.5 s" or "2 min". A setting kept in megabytes, seconds or hours keeps its unit, and its default and its range read in it ("Default 256 MiB · 8 MiB to 16 GiB"). A value that is refused says what is taken under the field. Counts (nodes, items, threads) and percents show a bare number. A theme's glow reads from 0 to 100 %. Saved settings keep their values.
