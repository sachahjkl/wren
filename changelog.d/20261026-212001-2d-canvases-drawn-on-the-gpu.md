**2D canvases drawn on the GPU** (`src/canvas2d/gpu*.odin`, `src/browser/canvas_gpu.odin`, `canvas.gpu.*` preferences; toolkit 0.26.0 [docs/canvas.md](ext/gui_toolkit/docs/canvas.md)): the drawing calls of a canvas are recorded and replayed in a few draw calls into a texture the page shows as it is, with no copy through the CPU; a `<video>` drawn into a canvas is taken from its GPU picture. Paths, gradients, patterns, images, clips, the 26 composite operators, shadows and `filter` are done by the GPU renderer; text is drawn from the glyph outlines as before. The software rasterizer stays: `canvas.gpu.enabled` off, a context made with `willReadFrequently`, a canvas under `canvas.gpu.min_pixels`, and a canvas read back too often (`canvas.gpu.read_penalty`) draw there, and the GPU path hands a canvas back to it when the renderer fails.
