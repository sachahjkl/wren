**A Messages setting for what the browser says**: about:settings, System, Messages chooses where messages such as "Copied", "Saved", "Deleted", "Imported", a failed save, an update found or a Vault notice go: the system's notifications (the default), the window only, or nowhere. A failure is always said. Hints about the window itself, such as how to leave full screen, stay in the window.
