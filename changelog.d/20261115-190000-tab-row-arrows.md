**Arrow buttons on the row of tabs when it overflows**: when the tabs do not fit, an arrow button shows on each side that hides tabs, and the tabs fade out beyond it. A click on an arrow brings the next hidden tab fully into view; held, it repeats. The wheel and a touchpad still scroll the row. A tab dragged to an edge of the row now scrolls it that way. A new or chosen tab still comes into view. In the classic look, while the tabs scroll, the new-tab button stands clear of the right arrow.
