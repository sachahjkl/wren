**`getComputedStyle` gives the used insets of positioned elements**: `top`, `right`, `bottom` and `left` of an absolutely positioned, fixed, relative or sticky element are pixels (a percentage against its containing block, an `auto` side where the box is, or the opposite side negated for a relative one) instead of the declared `10%` or `auto` that position libraries (tooltips, popovers, sticky polyfills) could not use; a sticky element keeps `auto`. Numbers of computed values are the shortest that read back the same (`20.7px`, not `20.700001px`). WPT `css/cssom`: 56 % to 78 %.
