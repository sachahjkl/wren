**Extensions open tabs and windows**: `chrome.tabs.create`, `update` and `remove`; `chrome.windows.create`, `update` and `remove`, where `type: "popup"` makes a window with no tab strip and no toolbar; `runtime.getContexts`, `runtime.reload` and `runtime.openOptionsPage`. The tab and window events (`onCreated`, `onUpdated`, `onActivated`, `onRemoved`, `onFocusChanged`) reach the extension.
