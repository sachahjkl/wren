**The WindowProxy of other documents is native** : a window of another document (a frame, the parent, the top level, `window.open`'s result) and its cross-origin `location` are exotic objects built with `JSClassExoticMethods` (`runtime/frames.c`) instead of the two `Proxy`es of prelude JavaScript (`prelude/frames.js`), trimming the realm image. What a cross-origin window exposes (postMessage, location, closed, frames/window/self, length, top, parent, opener, close/focus/blur, indexed and named child frames) and the pristine ECMAScript built-ins / platform objects a same-origin frame yields are unchanged; everything else still throws `SecurityError`.
