type: Changed

**Text settings renamed**: the text rendering settings are now under `appearance.text.*` (about:config), shared with the other programs of the same toolkit, and a change applies at once. Values set before under `font.raster.*`, `font.atlas.*`, `font.color_glyphs*`, `font.fallback.system`, `font.shape_cache_words`, `text.renderer`, `text.slug.*` and `text.emoji.font` are not carried over.
