**The look is the toolkit's common settings**: the theme, light or dark, the text size, the zoom of the interface, the animations and the system's title bar are now `appearance.theme`, `appearance.mode`, `appearance.font_size`, `appearance.zoom`, `appearance.animations` and `window.system_title` (they were `browser.ui.theme`, `theme_mode`, `font_size`, `zoom`, `animations` and `browser.window.integrated_titlebar`; values set under the old names are not carried over). about:settings also offers the density, the rounding and where messages show, and lists your own themes after the toolkit's: a link under the theme cards makes `themes.txt` in the profile and opens it. A zoom step of the interface (Ctrl+wheel, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-) multiplies the zoom by `input.zoom_step`.
