**A speaker on the tab that plays sound, and muting a tab** (`tab.mute`, `browser.tabs.audio_indicator`, `browser.tabs.audio_hold_ms`) : a click on the speaker (in the sidebar and the row of tabs), the tab's menu or the command mutes it without the page knowing (`media.player_set_host_muted`); `wren ctl tabs --json` reports `playing`, `audible` and `muted`.
