**Ctrl+F in the developer tools** (`src/browser/devtools_elements.odin`, `src/dom/xpath.odin`): in Elements, "Find by string, selector, or XPath" finds the nodes whose tag, attributes or text hold the query, those the query selects as a CSS selector (the page's own matcher) and as an XPath 1.0 expression (paths and axes, predicates, unions, comparisons, `contains`, `starts-with`, `text()`, `last()`, `count()`...), together in document order; it says `n of m`, Enter / F3 go to the next match and Shift+Enter / Shift+F3 to the previous one, the tree opens and scrolls to it and the page shows its box, the matches follow the document as it changes, Escape closes it. Ctrl+F in the Console, the Network and the Application panels takes their filter field. F3 and Ctrl+F there are the developer tools', not the page's find.
