**createImageBitmap options** (`resizeWidth`, `resizeHeight`, `resizeQuality`, `imageOrientation`, `premultiplyAlpha`, `colorSpaceConversion`) : every ImageBitmapOptions field is now honoured natively for all sources (Blob, ImageData, `<img>`, `<canvas>`, `<video>`, `ImageBitmap`, `OffscreenCanvas`), on top of the existing crop rectangle. The resize runs in Odin on the premultiplied bitmap with four qualities (`pixelated` nearest, `low` bilinear, `medium` box-reduced, `high` a separable triangle filter whose radius follows the scale, so any reduction is smooth with no level seams); an axis left at 0 keeps the source aspect ratio. `premultiplyAlpha` and `colorSpaceConversion` are no-ops against our premultiplied-sRGB storage and are accepted for completeness.
