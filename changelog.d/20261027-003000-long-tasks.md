**Long Tasks** (`PerformanceObserver` type `longtask`, `PerformanceLongTaskTiming`, `TaskAttributionTiming`): a task of a page's scripts (its microtasks included) that takes 50 ms or more is reported to the observers (`name` "self", attributed to the window; not in `performance.getEntries()`, as in Chromium); `PerformanceObserver.supportedEntryTypes` lists it. Preference `performance.long_task_entries` (200; 0 turns the timing of tasks off).
