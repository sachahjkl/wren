**The developer tools dock at the bottom of the window and move as every view**: F12 opens them as a panel at the bottom of the window. You can dock them at another edge, float them over the page, or put them in a window of their own, from their dock menu, their panel's menu or the command palette. Wren remembers where you put them. Closing their own window puts them back at the bottom of the browser window. Each browser window still has its own developer tools. The preferences `browser.devtools.dock`, `browser.devtools.size` and `browser.devtools.window.*` are gone.
