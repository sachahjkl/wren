**IndexedDB** (`src/idb`, `api/idb.widl`, `native/js/lazy/idb.js`, `native/js/runtime/web_idb.c`; `indexeddb.*` preferences): `indexedDB`, `IDBFactory`, `IDBDatabase`, `IDBTransaction`, `IDBObjectStore`, `IDBIndex`, `IDBCursor`, `IDBKeyRange`, `IDBRequest` and the events of IndexedDB 3.0, in pages **and in dedicated workers** (a worker is a client of its creator's origin: `versionchange`, `blocked` and the order of transactions work between a page and its workers, and a terminated worker's connections close). Kept per origin in the profile: `idb\<origin>.widb` is an append-only journal (one record with a checksum per committed transaction, a torn or damaged end is dropped at the next load) and `<origin>.widx` a checkpoint of the keys, validated by its checksum and rebuilt from the journal if it is damaged. **Values stay on the disk**: memory holds the keys and the places of the values, a value is read when a request asks for it (a cache of `indexeddb.value_cache_bytes`), object stores load when a transaction first needs them and idle ones are evicted, so a database of a million records / 1 GiB opens in under a millisecond and a first transaction costs 280 ms, instead of loading everything. Compaction (`indexeddb.compact_ratio`) runs in the background and never blocks a transaction; small commits are written by the committing thread and grouped, big ones on the blocking pool; `getAll` replies in pieces of 2 MiB. Requests are asynchronous (one task each), a transaction completes once its record is on disk (`indexeddb.durability = strict` also flushes), a failed write rolls the transaction back, and an origin is limited by `indexeddb.quota` (`QuotaExceededError`). The JavaScript of IndexedDB is not part of the startup image of every page: it is fetched and compiled on the first use of `indexedDB` (a page that never touches it saves about 600 KiB and 0.15 ms per realm), and it is only the glue (classes, the order of checks, events): keys, key paths, the frames of requests and the decoding of replies are native (`native/js/runtime/web_idb_native.c`), which took the prelude from 100 KB to 89 KB and made a request 15 to 45 % faster (`getAll` of 2,000 objects 5.1 to 2.8 ms). **Storage buckets** (`navigator.storageBuckets`: `open`, `keys`, `delete`; `StorageBucket` with its own `indexedDB`, `estimate`, `persist`, `durability`, `expires`) and `navigator.storage` (a real `estimate`, `persist`, `persisted`; `getDirectory` rejects: there is no origin private file system). **Clear site data** in the page-info box clears the cookies, `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, IndexedDB and the HTTP cache entries of the site, and `about:sites` lists every site with the size of each kind of data and a button to clear a kind or all. duck.ai stopped showing its "can't access local storage" banner. Limits: no `SharedWorker`, no cross-origin isolation features (`SharedArrayBuffer` keys), no partitioned storage by top-level site. The WPT `IndexedDB` folder passes 97.9 % of its subtests (windows and workers).
