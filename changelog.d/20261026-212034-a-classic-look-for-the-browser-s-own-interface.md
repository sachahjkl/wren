**A classic look for the browser's own interface** (`browser.ui.chrome_style` = `modern` (default, unchanged) or `classic`; `classic_slope_deg`, `classic_gloss`; commands `Appearance: classic tabs` / `Appearance: modern tabs`, also in the menu's View; about:config, `wren ctl pref set`, at once): tabs are trapezoids with sloped sides, rounded shoulders and a concave foot that flares into the toolbar's line, the current tab is attached to the toolbar (one gradient, no line between them), the others are darker, behind it and overlap by a few pixels, with gradients on the tab bar, the tabs and the toolbar, light and dark hairlines, a white sunken address field and a close cross on every tab. Not a palette: it composes with every one (the colors are derived from the palette's window and border colors, light or dark). All drawn (polygon clips and gradients, sharp at every scale), the shapes made once per size, nothing drawn when nothing moves. A tab's click and drag-the-window zones follow its shape (gui_toolkit 0.28.0: `Box.slant`, slanted holes). `src/browser/chrome_style.odin`, `docs/chrome-style.md`.
