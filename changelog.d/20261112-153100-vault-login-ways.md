**More ways into the Vault**: log in with a passkey (Windows; on Linux once security keys give their PRF output), or by approving the login on another device where you use Bitwarden, which shows the same phrase; ask for your master password hint; let the server remember this device for the second step. The login dialog opens straight from the Vault button, remembers your email after a logout, shows a spinner while it works, keeps your password after a refused login, and every secret field has an eye to show it. Logging out asks first.
