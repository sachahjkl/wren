**Pages in legacy encodings (Shift_JIS, EUC-KR, Big5, GBK, windows-1252...) link and submit in their encoding**: `document.characterSet` says the encoding a page was decoded from (it always said UTF-8; a document of its own, `createHTMLDocument`'s or `DOMParser`'s, is UTF-8, and so is an XHTML page that declares none, as XML wants), the query of its links (`a.href`, links a script or the user follows, against the `<base>` too) is percent-encoded in it, and forms submit in it (or in the first encoding `accept-charset` names): query, urlencoded, text/plain and multipart bodies, `_charset_`, `&#N;` for what the encoding cannot represent. A search form of a Shift_JIS site sent mojibake. Shift_JIS text with a lead byte between 0xE1 and 0xFB (the level 2 kanji, the IBM extensions) is no longer U+FFFD, in pages and in `TextDecoder`. WPT `encoding`: 2.4 % to 97.7 % of its subtests.
