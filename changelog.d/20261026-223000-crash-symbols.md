**Crash reports of a release name their functions** ([docs/crash-reports.md](docs/crash-reports.md)): a report says which build crashed (`build wren.exe <fingerprint>, base 0x...`: the PE header's link time stamp and image size) and gives each frame as an offset in its module; every build keeps the linker's map named by that fingerprint (`build\wren-<fingerprint>.map`, not in the package: an artifact of the CI and of `tools\experimental.ps1`), and `tools\crash_symbolize.ps1 <report.txt|.dmp>` names the frames from it (reports from before: the base and the fingerprint come from the minidump).
