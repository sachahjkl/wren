**`env()`**: a value with `env()` was invalid, so `padding-left: max(var(--gutter), env(safe-area-inset-left, 0px))` gave no padding at all (and Wren's own site no side gutter). `env()` is now substituted at computed-value time, as `var()` is, inside `max()`, `min()`, `clamp()`, `calc()`, shorthands and custom properties: the `safe-area-inset-*`, `safe-area-max-inset-*` and `keyboard-inset-*` variables are `0px`, any other name (`titlebar-area-*` outside a window controls overlay) takes its fallback, and `CSS.supports()` accepts it.
