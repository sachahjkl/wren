**Audio on Linux** (`media.audio.backend`) : decoded audio plays through the system's device (cpal over ALSA, which PipeWire and PulseAudio serve), with Symphonia decoding AAC-LC, MP3, FLAC, Vorbis and PCM and libopus decoding Opus; where there is no device, or when the preference is `null`, audio goes by in silence at the pace of the clock, as before.
