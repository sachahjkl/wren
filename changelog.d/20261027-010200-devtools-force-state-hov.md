**Styles pane `:hov`**: a `:hov` button in the Elements tab's Styles pane forces `:hover`, `:active`, `:focus`, `:focus-within` and `:visited` on the selected element (and its ancestors, as a real pointer or focus does), and the rules those states bring in appear in the list, as Chrome does. `:focus-within` can now be forced on its own.
