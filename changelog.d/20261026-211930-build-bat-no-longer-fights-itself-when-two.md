**`build.bat` no longer fights itself when two builds run at once** (`test_all -Jobs n`): `build\wren.com` is built only when a source is newer, and `bindgen` runs from an exe of its own that is deleted after; both were written by two builds together and one was refused (`Error(013)`). `test_all -Jobs` defaults to one step per core (at most 8) on a machine of ours and to two on the CI: measured on the runner (4 vCPU), two at a time take 514 s, three 539 s (and three checks failed under the load), four 765 s (the steps' durations add up to twice as much: they compile and test, they do not wait). The checks that failed under three are out of the CI now (a check that fails again under the load depends on the machine's speed and goes behind `-Perf`): the 20 000 and 50 000 nested elements of `jstest` (a load test under a 20 s limit) and the wasm preemption hook's tick count run with `-Perf`; canvas2d's stroke edge reads the strongest pixel of its row instead of one (not reproduced here in 40 runs with every core busy).
