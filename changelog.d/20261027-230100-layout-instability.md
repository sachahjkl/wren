**Layout Instability** (`PerformanceObserver` type `layout-shift`, `LayoutShift` with `LayoutShiftAttribution`, prefs `performance.layout_shift_entries`, `performance.layout_shift_recent_input_ms`) : each frame compares where visible, non-fixed boxes are to where they were and reports the unexpected movement of content as a `value` (the spec's impact fraction × distance fraction), with `hadRecentInput` and `lastInputTime` (a shift within `performance.layout_shift_recent_input_ms` of a click or key does not count), and `sources` (each a `node`, `previousRect` and `currentRect`). Boxes moved by a transform, in `position: fixed`, or off screen do not count. Session windows (1 s / 5 s) are kept internally for Cumulative Layout Shift. Computed natively in the rendering update. `PerformanceObserver.supportedEntryTypes` lists it.
