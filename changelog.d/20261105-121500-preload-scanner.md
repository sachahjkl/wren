**A document's requests leave while its bytes arrive** (`html.preload.enabled`, `html.preload.max_requests`, `html.preload.max_images`): scripts, stylesheets, images and `<link rel=preload>` found in the arriving bytes are asked for before the document is parsed, under its base URL, `crossorigin`, media attributes, referrer policy and Content-Security-Policy, and the elements then take these requests over instead of making their own. cnn.com's first subresource request goes out at 130 ms instead of 450. An `<img>` resolves against `<base href>` now.
