**The licence card's seal has a real relief and answers the lights** (`about_card_seal.odin`, shader `card-seal`, `card_foil.wgsl`): its face was flat. It is now lit by the card's own shader (a second entry of the registry on the same module, `seal` mode) with two textures made natively like the card's: a normal map of the medal (a reeded gold edge of 45 flutes rounded off at the rim, a smooth gold lip, a domed green face with fine concentric rings, a raised gold rib; cavity and gold mask in the other channels) and a raised-lettering map for the word `CERTIFIED` and the tick (the same bevel code as the card's text). The face, rings, edge and lettering respond to the key light, the sweeping light and the pointer (gloss tight on the enamel, wide and gold on the metal), the letters cast a shadow on the face, and the CSS layers stay as the fallback (shader unavailable: `data-shader=off` on the seal, reduced motion, narrow card); the seal's thickness (walls, cast shadow) is still CSS. The sweep animation runs on the seal's element too, in step with the card's; a card at rest still draws no frame.
