**An artistic new tab** (`browser.newtab.art.enabled`) : a new tab can show a random public-domain painting filling the window, like Google Arts & Culture's art new tab, with the start page's shortcuts and address bar over it and a discreet caption (title, artist, date, museum, a link to the record). A background task started by the first art new tab keeps a sliding window of about twenty paintings ready in the profile's temporary folder; it is courteous (one request per host, honest User-Agent, exponential backoff), validates every image (MIME, size, probe decode, ratio, a minimum width) and chooses one suited to the window's orientation. Sources are Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access (CC0) and Wikimedia Commons (freely licensed paintings only), replaceable through `browser.newtab.art.sources`. No network request is made until the first art new tab, none while it is off; nothing but the image and listing requests leaves the machine, and the cache is emptied by Clear browsing data. Off by default. See docs/newtab-art.md.
