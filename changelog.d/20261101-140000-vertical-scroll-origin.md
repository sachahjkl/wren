**Vertical text**: a document whose lines run upwards (`vertical-rl` or `vertical-lr` with `direction: rtl`, `sideways-lr`) starts at the bottom and scrolls up, with negative `scrollTop`; end margins count in a vertical or right-to-left scroll container; `@position-try` rules take flow-relative properties for the element's writing mode; `<meter>` and `<progress>` lie along their own lines and show their value.
