**Reference tests in the WPT runner** (`tools\wpt.ps1 -Reftests`, `css/css-multicol` and `css/css-writing-modes` for now): the test and its references laid out headless, drawn by a software rasteriser over the display list (`src/tools/wpt/reftest`: clips, transforms, opacity groups, backgrounds, gradients, images, shadows, borders of every style, Ahem exactly, other fonts as deterministic patterns), compared under WPT's rules (`<meta name=fuzzy>`, chains of references, `reftest-wait`). They count apart from testharness tests, with expectations of their own (`wpt/expectations/<folder>.reftest`), and the three pictures of a failure can be dumped outside the repository (`-ReftestDump`).
