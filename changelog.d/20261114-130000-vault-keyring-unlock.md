**Unlock the Vault from the system's keyring**: with the setting `vault.unlock.remember`, the Vault opens without its password when the browser starts and when you click Unlock, from GNOME Keyring, KWallet or Windows' Credential Manager: until the computer restarts (session) or across restarts (always). "Ask for the password next time" in the Vault's panel asks it once. Off by default; the key is only ever in the keyring.
