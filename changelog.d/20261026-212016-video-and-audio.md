**`<video>` and `<audio>`** (`media.*` preferences): MP4 (progressive, moov at the end, fragmented) and WebM are parsed in Odin and decoded by the system's Media Foundation decoders: H.264 and AAC, Opus, and where the system has a decoder (Store extensions) AV1, VP9 and HEVC, on the GPU through DXVA. Decoded pictures stay in video memory (NV12 / P010 textures of the renderer's device, converted to RGBA in one draw when a new picture came), so a video takes `border-radius`, `object-fit`, `opacity`, filters and transforms as an image does, and the page is not painted again for each picture. The sound goes to WASAPI and is the clock; threads decode ahead of it. `HTMLMediaElement` follows the HTML state machine (resource selection with `<source>`, `readyState` and its events, `seeking`, `timeupdate`, `ended`, `play()` promises, the autoplay policy `media.autoplay.policy`, `buffered` / `seekable` / `played`, `error`, `canPlayType`), with built-in controls (`media.controls.*`) and a demo page (`video.html`).
