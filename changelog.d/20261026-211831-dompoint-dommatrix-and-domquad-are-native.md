type: Changed

**`DOMPoint`, `DOMMatrix` and `DOMQuad` are native** (`native/js/runtime/web_geometry.c`, the members bound by the generator from the Geometry Interfaces IDL; `DOMRect` was already): the 4x4 arithmetic, the transform-list parser and the serialization run in the engine in C, with no JavaScript. `prelude/geometry.js` (18.7 KB of prelude) is gone; the JavaScript heap of a fresh realm is 40 KiB smaller (1282 to 1242 KiB) and the image 50 KiB smaller; creating a realm costs the same (0.7 to 0.8 ms). `DOMQuad` (`fromRect`, `fromQuad`, `getBounds`) exists for the first time, matrices are structured-cloneable and the canvas's `getTransform` no longer looks `DOMMatrix` up on the global object. Behaviour follows the specification where the JavaScript did not: `fromMatrix` validates and fixes up a `DOMMatrixInit` by value (a 3D member that is the identity's keeps a matrix 2D, `is2D: true` with another value is a `TypeError`), a matrix that cannot be inverted is 3D, `rotateAxisAngle` around z keeps a matrix 2D, `is2D` is read-only, `fromFloat32Array` / `fromFloat64Array` need a typed array of that type, a transform list refuses a unitless length or angle other than zero and relative units (`em`, `%`), `rotateZ()` makes a matrix 3D, a transform list in a worker is a `TypeError`, and `rotate(90deg)` uses the cosine and sine directly. New test: `examples/jsweb/geometry.js` (jswebtest `geometry`).
