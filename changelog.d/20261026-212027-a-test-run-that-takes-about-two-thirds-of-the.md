**A test run that takes about two thirds of the time** (`tools/test_all.ps1 -Quick -Jobs 2`: 7 minutes to 4 minutes 45 on the development machine, a third less; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)): every script that times itself prints the seconds of each of its sections, and `test_all` the slowest steps and sections at its end (and in the job summary of a GitHub run). `remote_e2e` drives one Wren for most of its scenarios, with `wren ctl reset` between them, and waits for conditions instead of fixed delays (a 9 MB request built by one call instead of a 9-million-iteration loop, 40 captures over one connection): 56 s to 26 s. The toolkit's pixel tests are five steps instead of one; the builds the server steps need run first and every other step runs with them in one batch; steps that mostly wait (`-Light`: the cache, network policy, content blocker, WebSocket and image tests) do not take a `-Jobs` slot; the browser is not built again when it is newer than its sources (`build.bat` takes 40 s even with nothing to do); WPT and test262 `-Quick` compile one module per package (5 s instead of 40); `asan.ps1 -Jobs n` runs the packages in parallel and the CI runs AddressSanitizer as a job of its own beside the others. `instance_e2e` (31 s to 12 s), `setup_e2e` (71 s to 46 s) and `resize_e2e` wait for the files, windows and states they ask about; `resize_e2e` drives one Wren for its three rounds; `crash_e2e` starts its crashing programs at once (26 s to 10 s). A Wren started by `wren ctl` on a throwaway profile no longer downloads the content blocker's lists.
