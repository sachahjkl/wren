**The wheel over an open menu scrolls only the menu**: over the main menu (scrolled in a window lower than it), its submenus, a context menu, the downloads drop-down, a drop-down list or a developer tools' panel menu, the wheel, down or sideways (a touchpad's), scrolls the menu if it can and nothing else: the page or a frame under it scrolled too. Toolkit 0.76.0 (`ui.menu_at`). Test: e2e `menu_wheel`.
