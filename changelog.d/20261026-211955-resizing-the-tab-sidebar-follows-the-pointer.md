**Resizing the tab sidebar follows the pointer frame by frame, one frame per refresh** (`tab_sidebar.odin`, `pacing.odin`, `page_frame.odin`): the sidebar and the page are laid out at the width the pointer gives in the frame that reads it (they were a frame behind: the handle's signal came after them); a drag of a panel inside the window (the sidebar's edge, the developer tools' splitter or floating panel) is paced as any drag, one frame per slot, instead of as a step of a window resize, which skipped the slot and made a frame per pointer move (505 a second measured with a 1 kHz pointer, most never shown, the others advancing unevenly); and the page beside a dragged panel is never painted at another width than its area's (the anchored paint of a long layout stays for window resizes). Measured with `tools\drag_fluidity.ps1 -Sidebar` (sacha.house/projects: edge behind the pointer 6 px → 0, no frame of the page at another width); test `tools\sidebar_resize_e2e.ps1`.
