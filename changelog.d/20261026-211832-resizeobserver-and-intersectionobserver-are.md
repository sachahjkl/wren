**`ResizeObserver` and `IntersectionObserver` are native** (`src/page/js_resize_observer.odin`, `js_intersection_observer.odin`; the engine keeps only the script's objects, `runtime/web_resize.c`, `runtime/intersection.c`): the observations live in the browser and are measured from the layout's boxes at each rendering update, after the animation frame callbacks and before the paint, with no JavaScript in the loop. `prelude/resize.js` and `prelude/intersection.js` (18.8 KB of prelude, no per-realm `getBoundingClientRect` / `getComputedStyle` round trips per target) are gone; the JavaScript heap of a fresh realm is 30 KiB smaller. Behaviour changes with it: the sizes are the layout's (a transform no longer scales them, a change of padding alone is reported), an `IntersectionObserver` target is cut by the clips of the boxes between it and its root (`overflow` other than `visible`, grown by `scrollMargin`), a non-finite `threshold` is a `TypeError`, an `IntersectionObserverEntry` can be subclassed, and the number of delivery passes of a rendering update is `javascript.dom.max_resize_passes` (4).
