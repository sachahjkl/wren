type: Changed

**The finish of the interface is a setting of its own**: `appearance.finish` (machined, edged, flat, gradient, glass) chooses how every widget is drawn, and `browser.ui.chrome_style` now only chooses the shape of the tabs, classic or modern. The classic tabs can now have the machined finish too. The former "machined" style is the modern tabs with the machined finish: a profile that chose it goes back to the classic tabs. `appearance.glow` (0 to 1) replaces `browser.ui.machined_glow`. The commands "Finish: …" of the command palette choose the finish.
