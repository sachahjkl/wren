**The finish of the interface is a setting of its own**: `appearance.finish` (machined, edged, flat, gradient, glass) draws every widget, and `browser.ui.chrome_style` only chooses the shape of the tabs, classic or modern. The machined style is now the modern tabs with the machined finish, and the classic tabs can have it too; `appearance.glow` (0 to 1) replaces `browser.ui.machined_glow`. The commands "Finish: …" choose it.
