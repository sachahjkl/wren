Modal dialogs and ::backdrop

A modal dialog (showModal()) is in the top layer, above the page and a card with its own z-index, over its ::backdrop: the UA's faint grey, or one the page styles. The backdrop covers the viewport and takes the clicks for its dialog: a click on it closes the tinted one.

A card with its own z-index: the dialogs and their backdrops stay above it.

The default backdrop: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1).

A blue backdrop. Click it to close this dialog.
