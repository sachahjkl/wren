# Wren 0.10.0

**Wren 0.10.0 brings the Vault's helper with it, and fixes what a new fuzzer found.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **The Vault works with nothing to install**: the Linux archive and the Nix package carry wren-vault beside Wren, and
  on Windows the first "Enable the Vault" downloads it beside `wren.exe` and checks it. A new Wren brings the version of
  wren-vault it was made with.
- **Safer with broken files**: a fuzzer now runs over what reads pages' content (HTML, CSS, addresses, images, fonts,
  media, subtitles, filter lists, extensions). It found a crafted AVIF image that stopped the browser, a filter list that
  read out of bounds, and leaks in subtitles: all fixed.
- **Something engraved on the back of the licence card**: turn the card over and look at it from the right angle.
- The tabs stand clear of the window's top edge, so the window is easy to grab above them.

### Upgrading

- The preference `vault.helper.path` is gone: Wren runs the helper beside it.
