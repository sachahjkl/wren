# Wren 0.8.0

**Wren 0.8.0 brings the Vault, a password manager that uses your Bitwarden® account, and browser extensions.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **The Vault**: log in to your Bitwarden account (the main servers, the EU servers or your own) and Wren fills your
  logins, cards and addresses, in frames too. It offers to save a new login or a changed password, generates passwords,
  fills the one-time code after the login, and signs you in to sites with the passkeys it holds. Unlock it with a PIN or
  from the system's keyring; it locks itself when idle, and filled values are never kept.
- **Browser extensions** (Manifest V3, Chrome builds): load one from its folder, a `.zip` or a `.crx`. Content scripts
  run in the pages and their frames, popups open under their button, and the background starts only when it is needed.
- **Security keys** with a PIN on Linux and NixOS, and a choice of account when a key holds several passkeys of a site.
- **A new default look**: the Ember theme in the machined finish under the modern tabs, menus with icons and a filter,
  buttons coloured by what they do, and dialogs with a header that remember their size and can float over the page.
- **The interface of the whole family**: the command palette, the developer tools' docking, the settings of the look,
  the text, the scrolling and the rendering, and the install's command line are the ones of the other programs.
  `wren --quit` closes the Wren that runs.
- **A log file**, and crash reports that name the session they come from.

### Upgrading

- Several settings were renamed or replaced by the family's standard ones (the look, the text, the scrolling, the
  updates, the rendering, the frame rate): what you changed is set again at its default.
- The command palette starts a new memory of your choices.
