# Wren 0.14.0

**Wren 0.14.0 no longer freezes when a video's decoder stops answering, and brings tighter dropdowns, steadier wheel scrolling and tabs that read as tabs in its panels.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **Closing a video never freezes the browser**: when the system's video decoder stops answering, closing the tab, loading another page or quitting goes on at once. Before, the browser waited for the decoder until it was ended.
- **Steadier wheel scrolling**: a wheel that keeps turning keeps scrolling the view, even when a field or a menu slides under the pointer.
- **Tighter dropdowns**: less room at their sides, and an open list is never narrower than its rows.
- **Tabs that read as tabs**: the tabs of about:blocker, the docked panels and the views stand on one line, the chosen one rising out of it. The browser's own tabs keep their look.
- **Readable find highlight**: a text found on a page keeps its colour under a light highlight.
