# Wren 0.7.2

**Wren 0.7.2 makes its window behave on Linux: it moves, resizes and maximizes from its own title bar, and an image copied in another program pastes into a page.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **The window on Linux**: drag the tab strip's free space to move it, its edges and corners to resize it, double click to maximize or restore it, right click for the compositor's window menu. Where the system draws no frame around it, it has a 1-pixel outline.
- **Paste an image on Linux**: a screenshot or a browser's "Copy image" pastes into a page (gui_toolkit's own PNG codec, which also reads JPEG and BMP).
