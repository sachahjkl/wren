# Wren 0.7.1

**Wren 0.7.1 fixes what the first days of Wren on Linux showed: sound plays through PipeWire, Invidious videos play, and `wren ctl` works from any shell.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **Sound on Linux**: the audio output opens through PipeWire's ALSA plugin, where every video and sound played silent.
- **Videos**: an Invidious video plays (XMLHttpRequest reads the body as it arrives), a video keeps its shape in its box, and the bands around it show the element's background.
- **Tabs**: closing the last tab closes Wren (`browser.tabs.close_window_with_last_tab`), and tabs that do not fit fade out at the edges of the strip.
- **`wren ctl`**: `eval` runs on pages with a Content Security Policy, `start` is headless on Linux, and `stop` finds a Wren started from another shell.
- **Nix**: `flake.nix` builds Wren from the sources (`nix profile add git+https://github.com/sachahjkl/wren`).
- **Text on Linux**: variable web fonts take their weight, and SVG text uses the fonts fontconfig gives.
