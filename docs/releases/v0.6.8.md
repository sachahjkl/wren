# Wren 0.6.8

**Wren 0.6.8 exports the frames it draws from its developer tools.**

This is still a beta, under development, and Windows-only.

### Highlights

- **Performance tab: Pictures and Export.** Pictures records the window's frames as pictures; Export writes a folder to the downloads folder: a page (`index.html`) that shows the frames big, one at a time or in a strip, with how much each changed and its time, kind, scroll and difference from the one before; the pictures and a contact sheet; `timeline.json`; and `trace.json`, the phases of every frame as Chrome trace events (the Performance tab of Chrome and Perfetto open it).
- `wren ctl timeline stop` writes the same `index.html`.
