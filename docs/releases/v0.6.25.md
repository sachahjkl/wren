# Wren 0.6.25

**Wren 0.6.25 opens the DOM of same-origin frames, lays out columns and vertical text, and sleeps when nothing changes.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **Same-origin frames**: `contentDocument`, `contentWindow.document`, `parent.document`; each frame keeps its own
  built-ins (`instanceof` across frames is false, as in other browsers); a frame's navigations fire their `navigate`
  event and keep their own session history.
- **Multi-column layout and vertical writing modes**: `columns`, `column-span`, nested columns, `writing-mode`,
  `text-orientation`, and documents that scroll horizontally or start from the bottom.
- **A GPU process for WebGL**, WebGL in workers and `transferControlToOffscreen`, WebGL 2 transform feedback and queries.
- **`AudioWorklet`**, and **P-521** in `crypto.subtle`.
- **A browser at rest draws nothing**: no frame and almost no wake on a page that changes nothing, and a caret that blinks
  redraws only its own pixels.
- **Faster and safer closing**: no wait on the shutdown watchdog, the graphics device let go before the end, `wren ctl
  stop` immediate on a throwaway profile.
- **The interface has its own zoom** (Ctrl+wheel over the tabs or toolbar), and its scroll bars show only when useful.
- **Crashes and hangs fixed**: WebGL while the GPU process restarts, an iframe moved into its own document, AV1 on a
  machine short of processors, large Web Audio graphs, a worker freeing a page's `localStorage`, WebSockets that stopped
  after a large message.
- **The User-Agent carries the Chromium tokens** before `Wren/<version>`, so sites that check for them (Google Sign-In)
  answer, and scripts get 30 seconds before they're stopped.
