# Wren 0.6.26

**Wren 0.6.26 runs pages that build large documents again much faster, and reads pages written in legacy encodings right.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **Pages that rebuild their DOM are much faster**: elements alike share their selector matching, `document` and `window`
  stay on the interpreter's fast path, a wrapper is one allocation, string arguments are read in place and a collection
  unpins its nodes in one call (a page that rebuilds 10 000 nodes every frame, measured on one machine: about 85 -> 142
  frames a second since 0.6.25).
- **Legacy encodings**: a page in Shift_JIS, EUC-KR, Big5, GBK or windows-1252 keeps its encoding for its links and forms
  (a search form no longer sends mojibake), `document.characterSet` says it, level-2 kanji decode, and documents made by
  a script keep their own encoding and URL.
- **`getComputedStyle` gives the used insets** (`top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left` in pixels) of positioned elements:
  what tooltip and popover libraries read.
- **`<dialog>`**: `beforetoggle` and `toggle` events, `closedBy`, and a modal dialog leaves the top layer when it is taken out
  of the document.
- **`document.createProcessingInstruction`**, and an error of a method of another frame's node is of that frame.
- **Text on several threads waits less** (the graphics toolkit's atlas and word cache locks).
