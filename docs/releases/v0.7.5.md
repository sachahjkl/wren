# Wren 0.7.5

**Wren 0.7.5 adds the bookmarks bar.**

This is still a beta, under development.

### Highlights

- **The bookmarks bar** (Ctrl+Shift+B, or a right click on the toolbar or a tab): the bar's folder under the toolbar, its folders as menus as deep as they go, reordered by dragging; the bar of a file from Chrome, Firefox or Edge becomes Wren's.
- **The launcher** offers New Window and New Private Window on a right click.
- **The downloads** show the system's icon of each file.
- **Type to search** on about:bookmarks and about:downloads.
