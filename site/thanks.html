Registering it

Open about:wren in Wren (menu, About Wren), paste the key into the box and press Register. Or drop the .wrenlicense file you just downloaded onto the Wren window.

The same key is sent to the e-mail address you gave at the checkout, with the file attached. Keep it: it works for every version of Wren, on every computer you use. A licence only supports the development and unlocks nothing. Wren checks it on your computer, without any network access.

Questions about a licence: open an issue on GitHub, with your order number (never the key). Back to the support page.
