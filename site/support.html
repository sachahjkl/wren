The payment is handled by Paddle, who sell the licence on behalf of Froment Software and handle VAT and receipts. Paddle's checkout is only loaded when you press the button. The key is shown on the next page and sent to the e-mail address you give.

What the licence is

It is a thank-you, not a key to a door. Wren is the same browser with it or without it.

A one-time purchase

You pay once. There is no subscription and no renewal.

Updates for life

The licence covers every future version of Wren, including the 1.0. The price may rise after 1.0; licences bought before stay valid.

Free during the beta

Every version is free and complete while Wren is a beta. A licence is a way to say thank you and to keep the work going, not a way to unlock features.

Nothing is nagging

No window pops up, nothing reminds you, no feature is limited. Without a licence, about:wren says “Unregistered — free during the beta”; with one, it says “Registered to” your name, with the date and order number.

Checked on your computer

A licence is a signed key. Wren checks the signature itself, offline: it never contacts a server about your licence.

The code stays public

Wren's source code is public under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 (versions up to 0.3.0 stay MIT). A supporter licence does not change what anyone may do with the code.

Once you have one

  1. Open about:wren in Wren (menu, About Wren), paste the key into the box, and press Register.
  2. Or drop the file. The .wrenlicense you received goes onto the Wren window, and that is all.
  3. To remove it, use Remove the licence on the same page.

The key is yours: it works for every version of Wren, on every computer you use. Keep the e-mail, or the downloaded file, in case you change computers.
