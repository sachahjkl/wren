Support Wren

Wren is a beta under development. Every version is free. A licence only supports its development: it unlocks nothing, and Wren works the same without one.

Coming soon

Licences are coming soon. There is nothing to buy today, and nothing is missing from Wren while you wait.

Prices include taxes. The payment is handled by Paddle, who sell the licence on behalf of Froment Software and handle VAT and receipts. Paddle's checkout is only loaded when you press a button. The key is shown on the next page and sent to the e-mail address you give.

What a licence is

A one-time purchase

You pay once. There is no subscription and no renewal.

Updates for life

The licence covers every future version of Wren, including the 1.0. The price may rise after 1.0; licences bought before stay valid.

Free during the beta

Every version is free and complete while Wren is a beta. A licence is a way to say thank you and to keep the work going, not a way to unlock features.

The code stays open

Wren's source code is MIT licensed and stays so. A licence does not change what anyone may do with the code.

Nothing is nagging

No window pops up, nothing reminds you, no feature is limited. Without a licence, about:wren says “Unregistered — free during the beta”; with one, it says “Registered to” your name, with the date and order number.

Checked on your computer

A licence is a signed key. Wren checks the signature itself, offline: it never contacts a server about your licence.

Once you have one

Open about:wren in Wren (menu, About Wren), paste the key into the box, and press Register. Or drop the .wrenlicense file you received onto the Wren window. To remove it, use Remove the licence on the same page.

Wren is developed by Froment Software. Questions about licences: open an issue on GitHub.
